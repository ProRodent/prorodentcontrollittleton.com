Rodent control for Englewood, no exaggeration.

Englewood is an incorporated home-rule city in Arapahoe County, the same county that covers part of Littleton, with its own city government and services separate from the county line.

Englewood sits roughly 6.3 miles from our Littleton base, and the inspection-first approach we use there carries over to every address out here too. Englewood's older bungalow blocks near Broadway and its newer multi-family development further south each bring a different mix of entry points.

Older housing stock mixed with newer multi-family growth.

Englewood's older single-family blocks, many dating to the mid-20th century, sit alongside a wave of newer apartment and townhome construction closer to the light rail corridor. That mix means an inspection here has to consider both settled foundation gaps on the older side of town and tighter, more specific utility penetrations on the newer buildings.

Properties close to the South Platte River on Englewood's western edge see more outdoor rat activity from the water and tree cover, while interior blocks further from the river see more mouse activity typical of any closely built residential area.

What people in Englewood call about.

The reasons people call from here repeat more than you might expect, and rather than guessing mouse or rat from a description, we check in person first.

  • Scratching or movement heard once the house is quiet.
  • Droppings noticed near stored boxes or a garage shelf.
  • An ammonia-like scent noticed in a basement or crawl space.
  • Chewed packaging, insulation or wiring discovered during unrelated work.
A 6.3-mile drive does not change how carefully we inspect a property.

How treatment and sealing work here.

What the walk-through finds decides the scope, but the approach itself does not change: trapping and removal first, then exclusion with steel, mesh or mortar to actually close the gap rather than just caulking over it. Price and plan are explained before any work begins.

Damage, whether it is insulation, wiring or ductwork, is documented with photos and billed separately under rodent damage repair rather than rolled into the main service price.

Response and scheduling here.

Calls from Englewood reach the same 24/7 line as calls from Littleton, with no difference in priority and a same-day visit offered where the schedule allows. Have your specific ZIP ready when you call.
