The honest picture of coverage near Denver.

From Littleton it is about 5.8 to 14.0 miles to Denver, and the visit itself follows the same three-step order used on every job we take. Denver's sheer size and age mean entry-point patterns vary block to block more than in any smaller town we cover.

A large city, honestly scoped to what is actually in range.

Denver has 39 ZIP codes in our coverage data, but only 32 of them fall within about 15 miles of our Littleton base, ranging from 5.8 to 14.0 miles out. The remaining ZIP codes sit further into the city, 18.1 to 23.5 miles away, and are outside our confirmed coverage range, so we do not list them here or claim to serve them.

Within the 32 ZIP codes we do cover, housing stock ranges from early-1900s construction near the city core to mid-century and newer development further south and west. The technician asks the building's age on the call and checks masonry foundation joints and old roof vents first on the older blocks, and utility penetrations and garage seals first on the newer ones.

What people in Denver call about.

Most calls from this part of our coverage area come down to a handful of recurring reasons, and we always confirm the species with an in-person look rather than a phone guess.

  • Scratching or movement heard in an older building's walls or ceiling.
  • Droppings found in a basement, garage or stored-goods area.
  • An ammonia-like odor noticed in an older building's crawl space.
  • Chewed wiring, insulation or packaging discovered during unrelated work.
A city this size does not get a simplified inspection just because it is a bigger market.
